🤝 Patriot Pantry - Food Insecurity Support

Free food and hygiene items for students in need

Program Overview

The Patriot Pantry provides access to non-perishable food and hygiene items to George Mason University students who are experiencing food insecurity.

Who Can Use It?

  • Degree-seeking GMU students
  • Enrolled in at least 1 credit hour
  • No income verification required

Available at Both Campuses:

📍 Fairfax Campus

Student Union Building 1, Room 3011

📧 Pantry@gmu.edu

📍 Mason Square Campus

Van Metre Hall, Room 211

📧 Mspantry@gmu.edu
